# Copyright and scope

`LICENSE` grants MIT over "the Software" without saying what that covers. Here is
the split.

**MIT.** The source code: `src/`, `scripts/`, and the root configuration files.
Fork it, adapt it, ship it commercially.

**Free to copy, no attribution.** The code snippets shown inside the articles.
Paste them into anything, commercial work included, with no permission and no
credit. This is looser than MIT on purpose: keeping a copyright notice attached to
a ten-line snippet is not something anyone does.

**Not granted.** Copyright (c) 2026 Geoffrey Migliacci, all rights reserved: the
logo (`src/app/icon.svg`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`, `src/app/favicon.ico`,
`public/icon-192.png`, `public/icon-512.png`, `public/icon-512-maskable.png`), the
articles in `content/`, their cover images, and the photographs
(`public/geofmigliacci.jpg`, `public/about/`). Quoting a passage is fine if it
names the author and links back to the original page.

Note that three of the logo files sit under `src/`, so "everything in `src/` is
MIT" would be wrong.

licenses: see [.agents/NOTICE.md](.agents/NOTICE.md).
